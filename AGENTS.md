# Infomaterial Observation settlement

This settlement is an infomaterial patch-assay die. Its internal circuits observe
one explicitly contracted regular-file, symbolic-link, or directory patch,
construct its placement-neutral deterministic typed-RMN Merkle identity,
measure its material, and emit a complete sorted RDF patch topology for
downstream cover, conductor assay, descent, and layout operations.

It does not assay or resolve conductors, claim a global instant or territorial
cover, compose patches, classify meaning, infer enterprise boundaries, design cuts, modify
the observed territory, follow symlinks, omit caller-selected paths, or act as
a registry. Those are different circuits owned elsewhere.

Its own `data/` is append-only RWIL/RDF evidence and content-addressed topology
artifacts. Scratch is outside the observed territory. The artifact is prepared
before placement; a missing provider seam refuses before observation, and any
later failure rolls back newly placed artifacts.
A patch scan closes before artifacts and receipt are placed, so it identifies
the exact pre-receipt patch state. Gap-free/nonoverlapping cover and descent are
different operations supplied by different dies; a volatile patch is split and
retried without invalidating receipts for stable siblings.
